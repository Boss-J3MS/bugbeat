Hear where your bugs are.

BugBeat reads your code line by line and plays it back as music. Clean lines keep the groove going; a line with an error bends the beat out of shape, so you notice the problem before you go hunting for it.

How it works

      
    
        
  1. 
          
    Write or paste your code
    
          
    Choose the language, or leave it on Auto-detect. Need something to try? Load a demo.
    
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          
    Analyze
    
          
    Gemini checks every line for syntax, logic and runtime errors and suggests a fix for each one. A machine learning model adds a risk score based on how complex the code is.
    
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          
    Listen, then fix
    
          
    Each line becomes a beat on the rhythm waveform. Click any beat to jump to its line.
    
        
    4. 
      

    


    

      
What you get

      

        

          
Fixes, not just flags

          
Every issue says what went wrong and gives you a corrected line to copy.

        

        

          
A waveform you can read

          
Green for clean lines, then orange through red as the problems get worse.

        

        

          
Your music or ours

          
Pick one of five styles, Lo-fi Chill to Rock Drive, or upload an MP3 or WAV and BugBeat distorts your own track on the buggy lines.

        

        

          
Run it right there

          
Execute JavaScript, Python, Java, C, C++, Kotlin or PHP and read the output without leaving the page.

        

        

          
History

          
Reopen any past analysis with its code, issues and rhythm.

        

        

          
Made for listening

          
Sound carries how serious each line is, so you don't have to rely on color alone. That helps auditory learners and programmers with low vision.
